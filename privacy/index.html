  4. 새 이야기·마음·답변 알림
  5. 신고 처리, 차단, 이용 제한 등 안전한 이용 환경 유지

  7. 출시 알림 이메일 발송(신청한 사람에 한함)

Privacy Policy

Posted: November 10, 2026 · Effective: November 17, 2026

Momento House Co.,Ltd. (the "Company") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws in operating tree-to-branch (the "Service"). This policy explains what information we collect and why, how long we keep it, and who processes it on our behalf.

Article 1. Information We Collect

Category Items Who

The Service does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

Article 2. Purposes of Use

  1. Sign-up, identity verification (SMS code), and login
  2. Recording stories: converting voice to text and creating titles
  3. Sharing stories, sending hearts, family connections, and follows
  4. Notifications about new stories, hearts, and answers
  5. Handling reports, blocks, and restrictions to keep the Service safe
  6. Usage analytics to improve the Service (using a service identifier only, without names or phone numbers)
  7. Launch update emails (only for those who signed up)

Article 3. Retention and Deletion

Information Retention period
Voice recordings Deleted immediately once saved as a story. Recordings not saved are automatically deleted 30 days after recording
Member information, stories, hearts, connections Deleted immediately when the account is deleted

Non-member records 1 year after last use

Information that the law requires us to keep is stored separately for the required period. Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be restored.

The Service is not intended for children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that we have, we will delete it without delay.
